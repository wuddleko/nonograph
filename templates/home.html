About Nostorgraph

×

What's this?

Nostorgraph is for writing things you want to exist somewhere permanent without that somewhere being attached to you, or to a single central entity.
A thought, an essay, a rant, class notes, up to you. You write it, you get a link. Share the link with whoever you'd like, or keep it to yourself.

{{about_durability}}

Notes are stored on nostr relays. They are reached through Tor, so nobody sees who posted a note or who read it.

Links

Store offers three addresses. A reader only has the one they were sent. The key is the part after #. This site never sees it.

Long. Most private. This site stores nothing. The event and the relays are in the address. The key is the part after #.
https://<site>/<locator>/<event-id>?r=<relay>#key

Short. This site stores the event id and the relays. It does not store the writing or the key.
https://<site>/<locator>#key

Title. Least private. This site stores the title, the event id, and the relays. The title is in the address. It does not store the writing or the key.
https://<site>/<title>#key

Principles

  • Posts cannot be edited or deleted after publishing
  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics
  • Open to all, no political or social biases
  • Open source, self-hostable

Source code

It's entirely open source. The code is at github.com/wuddleko/nostorgraph. You can read it, run it yourself, and host for others easily.
